# Question bank: restaurants and hospitality

Ask after the [discovery call guide](discovery-call-guide.md). Answers feed
[sow-restaurant.md](../templates/sow-restaurant.md).

## Service and capacity

| Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Service periods (brunch, lunch, dinner, late night) and holiday closures for each location? | Hours behavior |
| How many calls arrive at once during the rush? | Peak concurrency criterion |
| Which languages do guests call in? | Language switching |

## Reservations

| Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Is Reserve with Google live, without a payment step? | Booking from day one with no integration |
| Which reservation platform? Do you want a direct integration? | Stages 4 and 5 |
| Above what party size must a manager handle it? Deposits or card on file? Cancellation and no-show policy? | Thresholds and policy statements |
| Waitlist and walk-ins: how do you quote waits? | The agent never guarantees a table |

## Menu and ordering

| Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| May the agent quote menu items and prices? Where is the current menu kept? | Knowledge source and freshness |
| How should allergen questions be handled? | Always to staff; never an assurance |
| Do you want phone takeout orders? Which POS? Prepayment? | Part B scope |
| Above how many items should an order go to a person? | Complexity limit |

## Events and catering

| Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Who handles private dining and catering? Which inbox or number? | Capture and route |
| May the agent quote event minimums? | Usually no |

## Red flags

- The booking widget requires a payment step, so the agent cannot book through it.
- The menu changes daily with no single source.
- The venue expects the agent to confirm allergen safety.
