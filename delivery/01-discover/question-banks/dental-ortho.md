# Question bank: dental and orthodontic practices

Ask after the [discovery call guide](discovery-call-guide.md). Answers feed
[sow-dental-ortho.md](../templates/sow-dental-ortho.md).

## Practice and schedule

| Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| General, orthodontic, pediatric, multi-specialty? How many locations and providers? | Routing and provider mapping |
| Which appointment types can a new patient book (exam, cleaning, ortho consult)? How long is each? | Booking link (stage 3) or booking in the PMS (stage 4) |
| Hours per location, non-working days and time zone? What languages do callers use? What is the same-day cutoff? Who decides on same-day emergencies? | Hours behavior, language switching, emergency criteria |
| What intake is needed before booking (date of birth, insurance, referral)? | Intake fields |

## Emergencies and clinical limits

| Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| What does the practice treat as an emergency: pain, swelling, trauma, bleeding? | A critical criterion |
| Is there an approved after-hours emergency script or an on-call number? | The agent never improvises clinical guidance |
| Ortho: how should broken brackets, poking wires and aligner questions be handled? | Self-care steps must be approved by a doctor |

## Insurance and billing

| Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Which plans are accepted, in or out of network? | The approved list the agent answers from |
| May the agent quote any price (new-patient special, consult fee)? | Prevents invented prices |
| Should callers text photos of their insurance card? To which number? | Intake path |

## PMS and data

| Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Which PMS? Cloud or on-premise? | Whether part B is possible |
| Is a BAA signed or ready to sign? Who signs the PMS authorization for each location? | A hard gate for part B |
| Is there a test patient or a sandbox in the PMS? | Test bookings must not touch real patients |
| How are existing patients verified today (name and date of birth, phone)? | Verification criterion |

## Red flags

- The practice expects the agent to give clinical advice or triage.
- An on-premise PMS with no API access.
- No approved emergency script.
