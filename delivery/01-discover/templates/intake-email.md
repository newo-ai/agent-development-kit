# Intake email: before the discovery call

Send this to the customer a few days before the discovery call. The answers go into
[requirements.md](requirements.md) through the skill
[gather-requirements](../../../skills/gather-requirements/SKILL.md). If there is no call, the
email alone can start discovery; follow up on what is missing.

---

**Subject:** Getting ready for your AI agent: 10 minutes of prep

Hi [first name],

Thanks for choosing to set up an AI agent for [business]. To make our call on [date] count, could
you send what you can of the list below? Rough answers are fine; we will go through the details
together.

1. Opening hours for each location, including breaks and holidays.
2. The main reasons people call you, roughly in order of how often.
3. What should always go to a person, and to whom (name or role, number or email).
4. Anything the agent must never say: prices, medical or legal advice, promises.
5. The systems you use for bookings, scheduling, orders or customer records, and who manages them.
6. Your website, price list or menu, FAQ, and any call scripts your team uses.
7. Whether you want to keep your current phone number.
8. Who will test the agent before it goes live, and the date you would like to go live.

Please do not send passwords or API keys by email. We will set up access securely when we need it.

Thanks,
[name], [partner]

---

## After the call

- Run [gather-requirements](../../../skills/gather-requirements/SKILL.md) on your notes or the
  transcript. The transcript stays on your machine.
- Send the filled requirements to the customer to confirm
  ([customer comms](../../04-launch/templates/customer-comms.md), template 2).
