# Customer communication templates

Emails a partner sends to the customer during a delivery. Used by
[draft-customer-comms](../../../skills/draft-customer-comms/SKILL.md). The partner reviews and
sends every email; nothing is sent automatically. Replace every `[bracket]`. Never put passwords,
keys or callers' personal data in these template emails.

## 1. Kickoff

**Subject:** [Business] AI agent: kickoff and next steps

Hi [name], thanks for today's call. Here is what we agreed:

- The agent will [main job].
- First go-live: [first go-live mode, for example nights only], target [date].
- What we need from you by [date]: [inputs].

I will send the requirements summary for you to confirm by [date].
[partner contact], [partner]

## 2. Requirements for confirmation

**Subject:** Please confirm: what your AI agent will do

Hi [name], attached is the summary of what the agent will handle, what goes to your team, and what
it must never say. Please reply "confirmed", or mark any changes, by [date]. Then we prepare the
statement of work and the list of behaviors we will test together before go-live.

## 3. Weekly status

**Subject:** [Business] AI agent: status for the week of [date]

- **Overall:** [on track / at risk / blocked]
- **Done this week:** [items]
- **Next week:** [items]
- **Waiting on you:** [items, with dates]
- **Go-live target:** [date] ([unchanged / moved because …])

## 4. Invitation to the acceptance test

**Subject:** Ready for you to test: [Business] AI agent

Hi [name], the agent is ready for your testing. Please call [AI phone number] or use
[chat link, if chat is set up] and try each item on the attached checklist. Mark each one pass
or fail with a short note. There are [N] items, about [time]. Please finish by [date]. If
something fails, we fix it and send you only the items to retest.

## 5. Results of a test round

**Subject:** Test round [n]: results and fixes

Thank you for testing. [X] of [Y] items passed. For the [Z] that did not, here is what we changed:
[item: fix]. Please retest those [Z] items by [date].

## 6. Night launch

**Subject:** Your AI agent starts answering tonight

Starting tonight at [time], calls to [business number] outside opening hours go to your AI agent.
You will receive a summary of each call at [email or SMS]. To switch it off at any time: [steps].
We will review the first [2 to 3] nights with you on [date] before moving to daytime.

## 7. Day launch and approval

**Subject:** Your AI agent is live: please confirm

The night review went well: [one line]. From [date] the agent will [answer after N rings, or
answer first].

What it does: [two or three lines]. What it does not do yet: [known limitations].

If you are happy with this, please reply "approved" to this email. That is all we need to close
the launch. [Full form only: please sign the attached acceptance instead.]

## 8. Handover

**Subject:** Your AI agent: what happens from here

Partner runs it (usual):

- We review your calls every [week] and keep the agent up to date.
- To change anything (hours, prices, a new question), just email or call [contact].
- If something is wrong, contact [contact] at [number].
- In an emergency, to stop calls going to the agent: [one plain step, for example "call us" or
  "turn off call forwarding on your phone"].

Customer runs it: add how to switch the forwarding off, who reviews calls and how often, and how
to request a change.

## 9. Reply to a change request

This reply is only for additions outside the current scope; a defect in what was agreed goes
through the normal fix flow.

**Subject:** Your change request: [title]

Thanks for the request. It adds [what], which is outside the current scope. Effect: [timeline],
[commercial note]. If you agree, reply "approved" and we will add it to the plan and to the test
list.
