# Home services and cleaning

## Essentials

1. **Services**, and what is not done.
2. **Service area**: cities, ZIP or postal codes, travel fees.
3. **Hours** for calls and for jobs, and emergency service if offered.
4. **Prices:** fixed prices, estimates, or "a technician will quote". What the agent may say.

## Typical integrations

- **Field-service and CRM systems** (job management, dispatch, quoting): create a lead or a job
  request from the conversation. Use the session-ended webhook or conversation tools with the
  system's API ([integrations](../docs/integrations/README.md)). Check whether the vendor offers
  an MCP server.
- **Urgent jobs:** a transfer to the on-call technician, with clear criteria for "urgent".

## Pitfalls

- An address collected by voice needs confirmation: spell street names and repeat the number.
- Do not promise arrival times the business has not confirmed.
