# General (any industry)

## Essentials first

Verify these with the owner before anything else:
1. **Products and services**: what is offered and what is not.
2. **Opening hours**: every day of the week, with breaks and holidays, and the time zone.
3. **Prices**, and whether the agent may say them on the phone.

Settings:
- `project_attributes_products_and_services`;
- `project_business_working_hours`, `project_business_time_zone`;
- `project_representative_agent_providing_prices_explicit_constraint`.

Then go through the greeting, transfers, FAQ, policies and reports
([Finish the configuration](https://knowledge.newo.ai/agent-setup#finish-configuration)).

## Typical next steps

- Keep the business number with forwarding after 2–3 rings: [telephony](../docs/telephony/README.md).
- Send every conversation to the business's CRM or email: [integrations](../docs/integrations/http-rest.md).
- Large documentation (support centers, insurers, banks, telecom): use Knowledge Studio
  ([knowledge](../docs/06-knowledge.md)).

## Keep it simple

A standard setup needs only settings. Do not bring up scenarios, Vibe mode, the Workflow Builder,
custom skills or code unless the owner asks for something the standard setup cannot do.
