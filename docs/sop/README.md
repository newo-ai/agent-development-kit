# SOPs: steps a person does by hand

An SOP (standard operating procedure) is a short list of steps for you, the person: the few steps
your assistant cannot do for you. Your assistant tells you when you need one. Each SOP ends by
telling you what to say to the assistant next. The pages in [playbooks/](../../playbooks/) are
the other way round: steps for the assistant.

| SOP | You need it when |
|---|---|
| [Connect the Newo MCP](connect-newo-mcp.md) | The first time you use the kit, in each AI app you use |
| [Sign in to the CLI](sign-in-cli.md) | Only when your assistant asks: you sign in with your Newo account, no key |
| [Edit a scenario](edit-a-scenario.md) | The agent must handle a conversation differently and no setting does it |

Stuck at any point? Tell your assistant "I'm stuck".
